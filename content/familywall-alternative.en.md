## A FamilyWall alternative without ads

If you are looking for a FamilyWall alternative, ads are often the reason, and what you want is to know what the family has on. TidTil is a family calendar for iPhone that stays with events.

The app shows **no ads and no pop-ups**, and it does not analyse your personal data for advertising. Development is funded through the optional family subscription, not through your calendar data.

## No account, in your iCloud

TidTil has **no account of its own**. There is nothing to register, no email address to hand over and no extra password to manage. For its cloud features TidTil uses Apple’s iCloud and CloudKit infrastructure – the account you already have on your iPhone.

TidTil runs **no servers of its own for your calendar data**.

## What TidTil costs

On your own device TidTil stays free to use: create and manage events and see them in the daily overview, with no subscription.

The paid part is **family sharing**, which lets you invite family members and sync events with each other. TidTil Family costs €1.99 per month or €19.99 per year. One person pays for the whole family.

Try free for 14 days: a one-time trial, with no automatic renewal. The trial ends automatically – no need to cancel.

## What TidTil does not include

TidTil has no shopping list. If you want lists, chats and a complete organisation system, this is the wrong app. Many family apps bundle exactly that. TidTil stays with events.

TidTil does not import a calendar from another family app. If events are already in Apple Calendar on your iPhone, you bring them over in a few taps: profile menu, “Calendar import”. What comes across is the title, start and end, once, and the import is included without a subscription. Apple Calendar stays unchanged. Later changes in Apple Calendar do not follow automatically.

## Who this is for

TidTil fits if you want a family calendar with no ads and no pop-ups, and you do not want to create an account. You do not get a shopping list, and you do not get an import from FamilyWall.
