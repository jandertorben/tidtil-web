## A family calendar for iPhone that focuses on events

If what your family mainly wants to know is **what's happening today, when an event takes place and who it concerns**, you don't necessarily need task lists, chats or an elaborate organisation system.

TidTil is a family calendar app and family planner for iPhone that deliberately focuses on events. You can assign events to family members, sync shared events with each other, and keep personal events private.

## A shared family calendar on iPhone

With TidTil you can invite family members and sync family events using Apple's iCloud and CloudKit infrastructure. One family subscription covers your family. Children without their own iPhone can also be added as family members, so events can be clearly assigned to them.

Personal events can be created and managed on your own device for free. Family syncing is optional.

## Private and shared events in one app

Not every event belongs in the shared family calendar. That's why, when creating an event, you can decide whether it stays **private** or is visible to the **family**.

That keeps the shared calendar clear, while personal events stay personal.

## A daily overview instead of calendar clutter

TidTil puts the focus on today. If you like, a compact daily overview can appear as a notification each morning. You can also set an individual reminder for each event.

The reduced month view helps you keep upcoming events in sight, without losing the focus on everyday family life.

## A family planner without task lists, chats or feature clutter

Many family apps combine calendars, tasks, shopping lists, chats, documents and other organisation features. That can make sense if a family is specifically looking for that all-in-one solution.

TidTil deliberately takes a different approach: **organise events, get an overview of the day, then put the phone away again.** The app isn't meant to replace a classic all-purpose calendar – it's meant to give families a simpler, shared overview of events.

TidTil is a good fit for families who want a **simple family calendar for iPhone** and don't want to maintain yet another complete organisation system. If shared events, private events, reminders and a calm daily overview are what matter most, TidTil is built exactly for that.
