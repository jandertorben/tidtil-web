Thanks for using TidTil. If you have a question, something isn't working, or
you've had an idea – just write to us. We're a small family project and we read
every message.

## Contact

Send us an email: {{< email-support >}}

We reply as quickly as we can – usually within a few days.

## Before you write

One of these hints might already help:

- **Events not showing up for the family?** Everyone involved needs TidTil
  already installed on their iPhone and must have accepted the invitation. The
  invitation link can't install the app itself – install first, then tap the link.
- **Nothing syncing?** Check that a signed-in iCloud account is active on all
  devices and that iCloud is allowed for TidTil.
- **Reminders not arriving?** Allow TidTil to send notifications in the iPhone
  Settings under "Notifications".

## Privacy

How TidTil handles your data is explained on the [Privacy page](../privacy/).
In short: everything stays in your personal iCloud.
