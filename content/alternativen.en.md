## No ads, no account

Leaving another calendar app usually means looking for a family calendar with no ads, no ad tracking and no pop-ups.

TidTil is a family calendar for iPhone. The app shows **no ads and no pop-ups**, and it does not analyse your personal data for advertising. TidTil has **no account of its own**. Syncing runs through your iCloud. TidTil runs no servers of its own for your calendar data.

On your own device TidTil stays free to use.

TidTil has no shopping list. TidTil does not import a calendar from another family app. Both are spelled out again on the pages below, along with what can be brought over once from Apple Calendar.

## What TidTil Family costs

The paid part is family sharing, which lets you invite family members and sync events with each other. TidTil Family costs €1.99 per month or €19.99 per year. One person pays for the whole family.

Try free for 14 days: a one-time trial, with no automatic renewal. The trial ends automatically – no need to cancel.
